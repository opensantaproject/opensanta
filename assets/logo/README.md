# Logo

Official OpenSanta Project mark, from the final package approved by Gav on November 16, 2026.

The lockup is the hat, gold sound-wave beard, and “OpenSanta PROJECT” wordmark. The icon is the hat and beard bars on a solid green circle.

## Masters

Vector masters (crop, scale, and export from these; do not redraw the artwork):

- `opensanta-logo.svg` — the full lockup
- `opensanta-icon.svg` — the circle icon

The full final package also includes 1024px PNG renders (white, cream, and transparent), sized icons (512 down to 16), `favicon.ico`, and one-color black and white versions of both marks.

Centering rule: the mustache is centered on the santa (the hat brim), and the beard bars are centered on the mustache.

Colors: green #497D4B, red #E11221, outline/PROJECT gold #B8862B, beard gold #E5B464, icon disc #3E7244, white #FFFFFF.

Type: Figtree Bold (SIL Open Font License), converted to outlines.

## Site files

These keep stable names so the HTML does not have to change. They are exports of the same final artwork:

- `opensanta-logo.png` — lockup for the home-page hero, trimmed to the artwork with an even ~50px white margin and saved 900×1004.
- `opensanta-icon.png` — 512×512 circular icon with a transparent background, used in the site header (same file as the final package’s `opensanta-icon-512.png`).
- `favicon-16.png`, `favicon-32.png` — same as the final 16 and 32 icons.
- `favicon.ico` — 16/32/48/64, from the final package.
- `apple-touch-icon.png` — 180×180 solid square (the icon on the disc color #3E7244) for iOS home screens. The final package’s 180px icon is the same art with a transparent background outside the circle.
