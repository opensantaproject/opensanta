In formation

A free Santa voice, made by volunteers.

A classic American Santa text-to-speech voice that anyone can use, recorded with consent and owned by no one company.

Record your voice Opens Dec 26

How it works

1

Record

Volunteers record their own Santa voice from a shared script.

2

Publish

Reviewed recordings may be published as an open dataset under CC BY 4.0.

3

Train

Those recordings are used to train a Santa voice anyone can use.

Why it’s open

Santa belongs to everyone

His voice should be free, open, and owned by no one company.

An open dataset

It may be published under CC BY 4.0, including commercially, with credit.

A public Santa voice

A free, classic American Santa TTS voice for stories, games, and other lawful projects.

Where we are

The project is in formation. We're preparing the public home, the docs, and the consent process. Recording has not opened yet.
