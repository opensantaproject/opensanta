How it works

1

Record

Volunteers, 18 and up, record a short script in their own Santa voice, with clear consent.

2

Release

We check the clips and release one clean dataset under CC BY 4.0. Use it, just give credit.

3

Train

The voice trained from that dataset will be free too.

Honest expectations

For makers, not for everyone.

A hobbyist-tier voice: warm, friendly, classic American Santa. It’s made by volunteers, so it’ll improve over time.

  • It won’t sound like a big studio production.
  • If “good enough for a kid’s ornament” is your bar, you’re in the right place.
  • Hiring for commercial work? Keep hiring a pro. Voice actors bring skill, direction, and polish a dataset can’t.
  • We’re the hobbyist tier, not a replacement for real talent.
