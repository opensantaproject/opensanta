A community voice project

Free Santa voice for the masses

We're building a free, open, high-quality classic American Santa voice — recorded by volunteers, with documented consent, and owned by no one company.

The OpenSanta Project invites people to contribute their own Santa voice recordings. Those recordings will train a text-to-speech voice that anyone can use for stories, games, and other lawful projects, including commercial ones.

Where we are

The project is in formation. We're preparing the public home, the docs, and the consent process. Recording has not opened yet.

Our ideals

  • Santa belongs to everyone He's part of our shared holiday traditions, so his voice should belong to the people: free, open, and owned by no one company.
  • Consent comes first Every contributor is an adult who signs a consent form and records a short spoken consent clip. We don't train on a voice without that.
  • Your audio, your rights We only accept recordings the contributor owns, made for this project (or fully owned, with the speaker's written consent).
  • Open licenses The dataset is planned for release under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0). The model's license is still being decided.
  • No cloning real people without consent Don't imitate a famous Santa, an actor, or anyone else. Use your own Santa voice. We won't clone a real person without their consent.
  • No scraped or ripped audio We never use audio taken from YouTube, TV, films, podcasts, hotlines, stock sites, or other sources that ban AI training.
