Plan and timing

Roadmap

We're just getting started. The first job is to establish the project and gather consented voice recordings. Training and a public voice come after that.

Where we are

The project is just getting started. We're preparing the public home, the docs, and the consent process. Recording has not opened yet.

Updated 2026-11-16.

The five stages

  1. 1 · Formation — current Set up the project's home and rules. Docs, consent, licenses, the public site, and (when they're ready) a project email and the sign-up and upload forms. Recording is not open yet.
  2. 2 · Recording Collect consented Santa voices. Adults record the core set plus one line pack. Main recording window: December 1, 2026 through January 2027. Earlier recordings are welcome once submissions open.
  3. 3 · Dataset Review and publish. Maintainers listen, run quality checks, and prepare an open dataset under CC BY 4.0. First milestone: about 1 hour of accepted audio from about 12–15 people, with every pack covered by at least two speakers.
  4. 4 · Training Build the high-quality voice. The plan is a blended community Santa, not a copy of any one person, using StyleTTS2 as the main approach. Compute and training wait until the recordings are in. Details are in the training plan (printable version).
  5. 5 · Release A free Santa voice anyone can use. A good v1 aims for about 2–4 hours of accepted audio from about 25–50 people, in late winter or spring 2027. A smaller on-device voice can come later. The model is planned for release under Apache-2.0.

Timeline

  1. Now · mid-November 2026

    Awareness and formation Build the public home, finish drafts, and (before any public ask) set up the project email and forms. A soft start can raise awareness, framed as something people may do after their season if they're busy now.

  2. December 1, 2026 through January 2027

    Main recording window Recording opens December 1. Record whenever suits you; many Santa performers will have more time after the holidays. Goal: about 1 hour of accepted audio from about 12–15 contributors, enough to start training experiments.

  3. December 15–24, 2026

    Go easy Mid-December is many Santa performers' busiest week. Recording stays open, but we won't promote or chase people that week. A quiet listing of the sign-up link is enough, once it exists.

  4. Late winter into spring 2027

    Toward a good v1 Keep accepting careful, consented recordings rather than rushing. Target: about 2–4 hours from about 25–50 people. Training happens when the data is ready, not before.

Still open

  • The project email (support@opensanta.org) is set up. The project Google account, sign-up form, upload form, and private Drive archive have not been created yet.
  • A lawyer's review of the consent form and licenses before launch.
