OpenSanta Project

Share your Santa voice

Somewhere a dad is wiring up a talking ornament, or building a Santa his kid can actually talk to. Your voice helps give him one, free. Read a short consent and your Santa lines right here, with no apps to install and no sign-in.

  • Step 1: Quick mic check in a quiet room
  • Step 2: Read a short voice consent out loud
  • Step 3: Record your Santa lines in one relaxed take

Step 2 · Microphone

Check your microphone

Find a quiet room with soft furnishings. Prop your phone about an arm’s length away, and avoid earbuds or headset mics.

Your browser will ask permission to use your microphone. Your voice stays on this device until you tap Send.

Microphone access is blocked

  • iPhone / iPad Safari: tap aA (or the page icon) in the address bar → Website Settings → Microphone → Allow, then tap Try again.
  • Android Chrome: tap the lock/tune icon next to the address → Permissions → Allow Microphone.
  • Computer browser: click the microphone or lock icon in the address bar and allow microphone access.

Speak normally — aim for the green bar.

Step 3 · Voice consent (v0.6)

Confirm and record your consent

Please read what you’re agreeing to, check the box, and record the short statement below in your normal speaking voice (not your Santa voice).

What you’re agreeing to (OpenSanta Consent v0.6)

  • You are 18 or older, and every recording is your own voice.
  • Your lines may be used to train AI speech models and published openly under CC BY 4.0 (anyone may reuse them, including commercially).
  • Your voice gets blended with about 100 others into one shared Santa voice. A little of you will be in there, and that’s the point.
  • This is volunteer work (no payment or royalties).
  • Your spoken consent clip is kept private only to verify consent — never published or used for training.
  • You can withdraw at any time for future releases by emailing support@opensanta.org.
Read all 11 consent statements (v0.6)
  1. I am 18 or older.
  2. I am the speaker in every recording I submit (or I hold all rights and attach the speaker’s written consent to every item below).
  3. None of my audio is scraped, ripped, hotline/phone, stock-site, AI-generated, or an impersonation of a real person.
  4. I consent to my recordings being used to train AI / TTS models.
  5. I consent to my recordings being published publicly under CC BY 4.0.
  6. I consent to the public release of a model that may produce a voice resembling mine.
  7. I understand anyone may use the released dataset and model for any lawful purpose, including commercially.
  8. I understand withdrawal only affects future releases and trainings, not versions already released.
  9. I agree that credit via the project credit line + contributors list (in the dataset/model card and docs) is reasonable attribution; no per-clip credit is required.
  10. I recorded the spoken consent clip myself, and I understand it may be compared to my recordings to confirm I’m the same speaker. It’s kept private.
  11. I am not paid and this is not an endorsement.
